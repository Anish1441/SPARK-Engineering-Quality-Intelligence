# SPARK Final Demo Checklist — Through Phase 9

1. Activate the original SPARK dataset.
2. **Model Health**: show registry, applicability, drift, and calibration status.
3. **Lot Intelligence**: show lot/batch health and commonality evidence.
4. **QA Inspector**: show applicability → Data Trust → Safety → risk → explanation → Module A/B → rolling forecast.
5. Record a governed QA decision if desired.
6. **Decision History**: show ledger integrity, feedback governance, and offline review candidates.

## Wording discipline

- Reliability Risk Score: QA prioritisation index, not failure probability.
- Lot Health: population prioritisation heuristic, not failure probability.
- Commonality: statistical association, not causality/root-cause proof.
- Calibration Monitor: backtest/review signal, not automatic recalibration.
- Feedback Learning: offline engineering-review queue, not automatic retraining.
- SHA-256 ledger: local tamper-evident integrity aid, not blockchain or digital signature.
